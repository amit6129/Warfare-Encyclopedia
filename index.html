World War II Wing

A World at War

The Second World War began in Europe on 1 September 1939, when Germany invaded Poland, and in Asia had roots stretching back to Japan's invasion of Manchuria in 1931 and full-scale war with China from 1937. It grew into the deadliest conflict in human history, drawing in more than 100 nations and fought across Europe, North Africa, the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans, and East and Southeast Asia.

Two broad coalitions faced one another: the Allied Powers, led principally by the United Kingdom, the Soviet Union, the United States and China, against the Axis Powers, led by Germany, Japan and Italy. The war ended in Europe on 8 May 1945 (V-E Day) following Germany's surrender, and in the Pacific on 15 August 1945 (V-J Day) after Japan's surrender, formally signed on 2 September 1945.

This archive documents World War II battles and engagements, 18 military operations, 16 key leaders and commanders, and dozens of weapons systems across every theatre of the conflict.
