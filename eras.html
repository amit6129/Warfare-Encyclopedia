Explore the Eras

Journey through history's greatest military civilizations — from the phalanxes of Macedon to the armoured divisions of World War II.

334 – 323 BCE

Alexander the Great

The Macedonian conqueror who created the largest empire the ancient world had ever seen — stretching from Greece to the Indus River.

Explore →

Key Battles

GranicusIssusSiege of TyreGaugamelaHydaspes

Military Innovation

Macedonian Phalanx with 5.5m sarissa pikes combined with Companion Cavalry shock charges. Alexander pioneered combined-arms warfare — integrating heavy infantry, shock cavalry, siege engines and light troops into a unified tactical system.

3rd – 1st Century BCE

Roman Republic

From the Punic Wars against Carthage to Caesar's conquest of Gaul — the legions that built the greatest empire of the ancient world.

Explore →

Key Battles

CannaeZamaAlesia

Key Commanders

Hannibal Barca, Scipio Africanus, Julius Caesar — three of the most studied military commanders in history. Hannibal's double envelopment at Cannae remains a foundational study in military academies worldwide.

1645 – 1818 CE

Maratha Empire

Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's Hindavi Swarajya — guerrilla warfare, hill forts, and the rise of an indigenous Indian military power.

Explore →

Key Battles

PratapgadPavan KhindSinhagadSalherPanipat III

Military Innovation

Ganimi Kava (guerrilla warfare), strategic use of Western Ghats hill forts, rapid cavalry raids, India's first indigenous navy, and intelligence networks — Shivaji Maharaj created a military system perfectly adapted to the Deccan terrain.

1796 – 1815 CE

Napoleonic Wars

Napoleon Bonaparte's Grande Armée and the wars that reshaped Europe — from Austerlitz to Waterloo.

Explore →

Key Battles

AusterlitzTrafalgarJenaBorodinoLeipzigWaterloo

Military Innovation

Corps d'Armée system, massed artillery doctrine, the decisive battle concept, and the Napoleonic column. Napoleon's operational art — moving independent corps that could combine rapidly for battle — revolutionised European warfare.
